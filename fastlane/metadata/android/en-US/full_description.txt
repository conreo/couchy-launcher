A lightweight home launcher for Android TV & Google TV.

No ads. No tracking. No accounts. Just your apps.

Features

• Carousel, Grid, or Dock layout  
• Video wallpapers from Apple/Amazon aerials (opt-in)  
• VPN status icon with quick-launch  
• Clock, date, network, and VPN in the status bar  
• Organize apps into custom sections  
• Hide apps, toggle labels and category names  
• UI scaling and wallpaper dimming  
• 17 languages  
• Fully D-pad — no touch needed  

Privacy

Zero network traffic by default. Video wallpapers are disabled until you turn them on. No analytics, no telemetry, no background services.
